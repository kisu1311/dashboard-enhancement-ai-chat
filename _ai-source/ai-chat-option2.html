system.cpu.percent

    
Description:

    
Measures the percentage of total CPU time in use across all cores. Expressed as a percentage from 0% (idle) to 100% (fully utilized). Helps monitor server load, performance, and capacity.

    
Interpretation

    
High Values: Usage above 80% indicates CPU saturation and potential performance issues.

    
Low Values: Below 5% means the CPU is mostly idle, which is normal during off-peak times.

    
Rca — When High

    
• CPU-intensive processes  • Runaway threads  • Insufficient capacity
